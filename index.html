SWCS Vision & Mission

A Seamless Gateway for Business in Naveen Nagpur

The Single Window Clearance System (SWCS) is NMRDA's commitment to a friction-free regulatory environment — where every approval, licence, and NOC for the Naveen Nagpur IBFC is delivered digitally, transparently, and within defined timelines.

Our Vision

To make Naveen Nagpur the fastest city in India for business approvals — a greenfield IBFC where regulatory clearances are measured in days, not months, setting a national benchmark for ease of doing business.

Our Mission

To eliminate bureaucratic friction by integrating all regulatory authorities on a single digital platform — ensuring real-time status tracking, defined service delivery timelines, and complete accountability in the approval process.

About Naveen Nagpur

India's Next-Generation International Business & Finance Centre

Naveen Nagpur is a 692-hectare greenfield township being developed by the Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority (NMRDA) as a planned International Business and Finance Centre (IBFC). Strategically located in central India, it is positioned to become a world-class destination for financial services, IT, and manufacturing enterprises.

The township is designed as an eco-smart city with underground utility infrastructure, 25% mandatory green cover, dedicated power supply, and multi-modal transport connectivity — including Nagpur Metro Line 2, Samruddhi Mahamarg Expressway, and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport.

Total Area 692 Hectares
Investment Target ₹8,200 Crore
Allottable Land 400+ Hectares
Mandatory Green Cover Minimum 25%

Why Invest Here

Infrastructure at Naveen Nagpur IBFC

The Naveen Nagpur IBFC has been planned with integrated infrastructure to support immediate business establishment. The following facilities are available to allottees from the date of possession.

Utility Infrastructure

Underground Utility Tunnel

Underground utility tunnels of 3.5m by 2.5m run beneath all internal roads of the township, carrying power, telecom, water, and gas lines. Road surfaces are not disturbed for utility maintenance or upgradation.

Length: 42 km of utility corridors
Services: HT/LT Power, OFC, Water, Gas
Standard: Trenchless installation, ISO 9001
Access: 24/7 SCADA-monitored manholes
Plot Allotment

Service-Ready Plots for Allotment

Plots are pre-certified under applicable Development Control Regulations. Electricity, water, sewerage, optical fibre, and piped gas connections are available at the plot boundary from the date of possession.

Total Area: 692 hectares master plan
Allocated: 400+ Ha ready for allotment
Plot Range: 500 sqm to 50 acres
Pre-approved: DCR norms pre-certified
Connectivity

Transport Connectivity

The project site is located within proximity of Nagpur Metro Line 2, the Samruddhi Mahamarg Expressway, and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur.

Metro: Nagpur Metro Line 2 (0.8 km)
Highway: Samruddhi Mahamarg (2 km)
Airport: VVIP Airport (12 km)
Rail: Nagpur Junction (18 km)
Green Standards

Green Township and Environmental Compliance Standards

The master plan has been prepared to conform with IGBC Green Township standards. A minimum of 25 percent green cover is mandatory across the township, with rainwater harvesting, solar-ready infrastructure, and zero liquid discharge systems at the precinct level.

Green Cover: 25% minimum, 173+ Ha parks
Solar: 150 MW rooftop solar capacity
Waste: Automated Pneumatic Collection
Water: STP + ZLD for all industrial zones
Digital Infrastructure

Power Supply and Digital Infrastructure

A dedicated 400 kV grid sub-station provides assured power supply to the IBFC zone. The township has a 100 Gbps redundant optical fibre ring and a public Wi-Fi network across all common areas.

Power: 400 kV sub-station, 99.9% uptime SLA
Fibre: 100 Gbps redundant OFC ring
Wi-Fi: City-wide 10,000+ access points
Data Centre: Tier III+ DC zone earmarked
