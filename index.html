How it works

Four steps from intent to operational.

Each step is designed to take minutes, not weeks. The platform does the discovery work, the integration work, and the tracking work — so investors and CA/CS professionals can move.

  1. 1 Step one

    Onboard in minutes

    Register with email + OTP. CAPTCHA-protected, foreign-national friendly, no document uploads at this stage.

  2. 2 Step two

    Identify approvals

    A conversational bot maps your sector, scale, and operations against our matrix to surface the exact approvals you need.

  3. 3 Step three

    Pay & get assigned

    Review fees, pay once, and a CA/CS is assigned to your case. The Common Application Form unlocks with auto-filled data.

  4. 4 Step four

    Track to completion

    Every application visible in real time, with SLA timers, clarification requests, and one-click escalation when needed.
